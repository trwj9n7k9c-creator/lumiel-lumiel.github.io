Lumiel Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 20th, 2025

Lumiel (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Lumiel collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Lumiel mobile application (the “App”).

Lumiel is a wellness application that provides sleep analytics, activity insights, daylight exposure tracking, and AI-generated wellness messages. Lumiel integrates with Apple HealthKit and uses your device’s health data solely to provide these features.

By using Lumiel, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Health Data (via Apple HealthKit)

With your permission, Lumiel accesses the following Apple Health data types:

  • Sleep Analysis (start time, end time, minutes slept, sleep stages including core, deep, REM, and awake)
  • Resting Heart Rate
  • Active Energy Burned
  • Distance Walking + Running
  • Apple Exercise Time
  • Activity Summary (including standing hours)
  • Time in Daylight
  • Date of Birth

What We Store on Our Servers

We do not store raw HealthKit samples. We store only:

  • Sleep start and end timestamps
  • Total minutes slept
  • Minutes spent in each sleep stage
  • Daily aggregated totals for:
    • Active energy burned
    • Distance walked/running
    • Exercise minutes
    • Standing hours
    • Daylight minutes
  • Derived metrics, including:
    • Sleep score
    • Activity score
    • Additional computed analytics used for insights

No raw workout samples, moment-by-moment heart rate data, or raw HealthKit datasets are uploaded.

What Remains On Your Device

No HealthKit data is stored exclusively on-device. All HealthKit data is processed into aggregated metrics before being sent to our servers.

How We Use Health Data

We use your HealthKit data only to provide core app functionality:

  • Calculating sleep and activity scores
  • Providing wellness insights
  • Displaying historical trends
  • Personalizing reminders
  • Generating AI-assisted messages using your aggregated metrics
  • Determining age-based sleep benchmarks (via date of birth)

Lumiel does not use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or data brokerage.

1.2 Account Information

Lumiel uses Sign in with Apple for authentication. We do not store your email address, real or relay. We store only the anonymous Apple user identifier needed to create and maintain your account.

1.3 App Usage Information

Lumiel does not use any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising trackers, or profiling tools. We do not collect behavioral tracking information.

1.4 AI Message Generation

When generating AI-powered insights, Lumiel sends the following aggregated data to the OpenAI API:

  • Daily and multi-day aggregated sleep metrics
  • Daily and multi-day activity metrics
  • Resting heart rate values
  • Derived scores and computed analytics
  • General “yesterday/today” flags (date tags)

We do not send:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Apple ID
  • Age
  • Timezone
  • Exact sleep timestamps
  • Any identifiers linking data back to you

AI outputs are for general wellness support only and do not constitute medical advice.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for the following purposes:

  • To compute wellness metrics (sleep score, activity score, daylight metrics, etc.)
  • To display health insights and trends
  • To generate personalized wellness messages
  • To personalize reminders and sleep-consistency tools
  • To provide account access and maintain secure authentication

We do not:

  • Sell your data
  • Rent your data
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Use your data for targeted advertising
  • Transfer health data to data brokers

3. Data Storage, Security & Location

3.1 Storage Location

All user data is stored in Supabase, hosted on AWS US-East.

3.2 Security Measures

Supabase provides:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS)
  • Encryption at rest
  • Row-Level Security (RLS), ensuring each user can access only their own data

We implement least-privilege access and secure storage practices.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data until you delete your account or request deletion. If you delete your account:

  • Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Deletion may occur sooner, but never later than 30 days
  • You may also contact us to request manual deletion

5. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Request a copy of your data
  • Correct inaccuracies
  • Delete your data
  • Withdraw consent
  • Object to certain types of processing

Requesting a Copy of Your Data

You may request an exported copy of your stored data by emailing: hello@lumiel.app

Requests will be completed within a reasonable time.

6. Children’s Privacy

Lumiel is not intended for users under 13 years old outside the EU/EEA. In the EU/EEA, Lumiel is not intended for users under 16 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these age limits.

7. Third-Party Services We Use

Lumiel uses only the following third-party services:

Supabase

For database storage and user authentication.

OpenAI API

For generating wellness insights using aggregated, non-identifying data.

We do not share personal data with any other third parties.

8. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We may disclose your information only in the following cases:

  • To comply with applicable laws or legal processes
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of Lumiel or users
  • With your explicit consent

We never sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

9. Security

Although no system is perfectly secure, we implement technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption
  • Row-Level Security (RLS)
  • Secure authentication
  • Limited access controls

You are responsible for keeping your device secure.

10. International Users

By using Lumiel, you understand your data is processed in the United States. Users in the EU/EEA retain rights under the GDPR, including access, correction, deletion, and objection rights.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the “Last Updated” date above. Significant changes may be communicated within the App.
